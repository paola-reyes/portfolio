AI-Promotora

A digitally synthesized person created to help members of the Latinx community learn about Alzheimer's disease.

Overview

This app is designed to be culturally relevant and accessible to the community, and it covers topics such as mental health, caregiving tips, and chronic disease management.

Our Research

Designing an accesible and user-friendly interface.

I built the AI-PROMOTORA web app interface to align with WCAG accessibility guidelines, ensuring a user-friendly experience for all community members.

Working directly with caregivers and community members to support their needs.

I engaged directly with caregivers to understand their challenges, moderating focus groups and interviews to gather qualitative feedback and design tailored resources.

Publishing our research.

I co-authored the research paper “You Believe That You’re Talking to a Real Person”: Design and Development of AI-Promotora for Latino Dementia Patient Caregivers (2026) to the Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction journal to outline the implications of our AI tools.

Collaborating with coalitions to support Latino caregivers.

This initiative is in partnership with RUSH University Medical Center and the Latino Alzheimer’s Coalition for Advocacy, Research and Education (LACARE) . We worked together to address disparities in Alzheimer's disease awareness and caregiving support within the Latino community.
